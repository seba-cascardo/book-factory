# Arcs — [title]

## Thematic arcs
One or two load-bearing themes, each a question the book explores. More
competing themes clutter the book; everything else serves these.

## Relational arcs
Stages are earned by events, never narrated ("they were warming to each
other" skips a stage). A usual ladder, adapted per arc: antagonism → thawing
→ commonality → trust → setback → working together → final test →
resolution (a bond, or its permanent absence).
